# UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Impact Mapping

**Project:** AquaSafe AI  
**Evaluation Standard:** United Nations 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development  
**Primary Goal:** SDG 6 (Clean Water and Sanitation)  
**Secondary Goal:** SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-Being)  

---

## 1. Primary Alignment: SDG 6 — Clean Water and Sanitation

> *"Ensure availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all."*

### Official Target 6.1:
- **Target Text:** *"By 2030, achieve universal and equitable access to safe and affordable drinking water for all."*
- **Official UN Indicator 6.1.1:** *Proportion of population using safely managed drinking water services.*
- **AquaSafe AI Direct Contribution:**
  - Traditional safe drinking water verification is hindered by laboratory latency (2–3 days) and prohibitive testing fees ($25–$80 per test).
  - AquaSafe AI provides near-instantaneous (< 5s) potability classification from raw physicochemical sensor values, enabling rural and peri-urban water supply managers to certify safe boreholes or instantly restrict unsafe kiosks before water is consumed.

### Official Target 6.3:
- **Target Text:** *"By 2030, improve water quality by reducing pollution, eliminating dumping and minimizing release of hazardous chemicals and materials, halving the proportion of untreated wastewater and substantially increasing recycling and safe reuse globally."*
- **Official UN Indicator 6.3.2:** *Proportion of bodies of water with good ambient water quality.*
- **AquaSafe AI Direct Contribution:**
  - Identifies hazardous chemical excursions including dangerous Trihalomethanes (carcinogenic chlorine byproducts), extreme Total Dissolved Solids (TDS), elevated sulfates, and abnormal pH swings.
  - Generates targeted chemical treatment plans (e.g. Granular Activated Carbon adsorption, membrane demineralization, and calcite neutralizing passivation) to eliminate hazardous pollutants prior to water distribution.

### Official Target 6.b:
- **Target Text:** *"Support and strengthen the participation of local communities in improving water and sanitation management."*
- **AquaSafe AI Direct Contribution:**
  - Bridges the technical knowledge gap by converting esoteric chemical figures (e.g., "Turbidity 7.8 NTU, Sulfate 420 mg/L") into understandable, vernacular-friendly household actions (e.g., flocculation settling using Moringa seeds, multi-layer cloth filtration, open boiling).
  - Empowers non-expert civic science volunteers and local well committees to take immediate corrective stewardship.

## 2. Secondary Alignment: SDG 3 — Good Health and Well-Being

> *"Ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for all at all ages."*

### Official Target 3.9:
- **Target Text:** *"By 2030, substantially reduce the number of deaths and illnesses from hazardous chemicals and air, water and soil pollution and contamination."*
- **Official UN Indicator 3.9.2:** *Mortality rate attributed to unsafe water, unsafe sanitation and lack of hygiene (exposure to unsafe Water, Sanitation and Hygiene for All (WASH) services).*
- **AquaSafe AI Direct Contribution:**
  - Contaminated drinking water causes an estimated 505,000 diarrheal deaths each year worldwide (WHO Global Health Observatory).
  - By providing pre-emptive water safety risk scoring and early warning alerts, AquaSafe AI halts exposure to pathogen-shielding turbid water, toxic chemical concentrations, and microbial vectors before clinical illness manifests.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for Impact Tracking

1. **Volume of Water Screened:** Cumulative volume (liters/day) from sources verified potable or safely remediated.
2. **Diagnostic Turnaround Acceleration:** Reduction in time required from sample collection to treatment issuance (baseline: 48h $\to$ target: < 1 min).
3. **Remediation Actionability Index:** Proportion of non-potable diagnoses accompanied by clear, executed treatment interventions (target: 100%).
4. **WHO Parameter Compliance Ratio:** Percentage of monitored points achieving WQI > 70 through corrective remediation.
