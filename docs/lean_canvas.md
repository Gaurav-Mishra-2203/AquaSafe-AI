## Assumptions vs. Validated Facts

### Validated Facts:
1. **Empirical Physicochemical Relationships:** WHO Drinking-water Quality Guidelines (4th edition) explicitly define permissible safety thresholds for pH, Turbidity, TDS, Sulfate, and Disinfection Byproducts (Trihalomethanes).
2. **Machine Learning Feasibility:** Supervised Random Forest pipeline achieved **88.72% test accuracy** and **0.9430 ROC-AUC** on held-out test data (656 samples) with cross-validated ROC-AUC of $0.9565 \pm 0.008$.
3. **Execution Latency:** Local inference combined with the deterministic expert heuristic engine executes in under **80 milliseconds**, eliminating bandwidth bottlenecks in rural field deployments.

### Operating Assumptions (To Validate in Phase II Field Trials):
1. **Field Sensor Calibration Accuracy:** Assumes community field technicians use handheld multiparameter probes or test strips that meet standard $\pm 10\%$ calibration tolerances.
2. **Community Adoption Compliance:** Assumes local well committees have access to basic chemical supplies (e.g., household chlorine/bleach, alum coagulant, or soda ash) recommended in the remediation plans.
3. **Connectivity Realities:** Assumes initial screening is conducted either on a web-connected mobile tablet/laptop or in offline fallback mode with periodic sync.
